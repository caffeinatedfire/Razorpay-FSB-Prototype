## Decisions for G2

1. **Hindi and Hinglish caption variants:** not drafted (the plan's cut item for this phase). The Hinglish *message* still appears on screen at 30 to 55 s.
2. **The plan's title card shows "H1 with its n".** H1 came out weak (14% of 37: most reviewers already use a tool), so putting it on screen would undercut the opening line. I propose the honest count of what was coded instead. If you prefer H1 on screen, the caption becomes "Only 14% of 37 reviewers chase by hand; most already use apps." That is true but changes the story.
