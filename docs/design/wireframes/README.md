# Wireframes (390 × 844)

Static HTML with synthetic data, rendered to PNG by `node scripts/render-wireframes.mjs`. Names of customers and businesses are invented. Phone numbers follow the fictional pattern `+91 00000 000NN`.

| PNG | Screen | Caption: the Track 1 element it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `s1-home.png` | S1 Home | **Who and what, and why mobile:** what is owed today and who to chase first, each with a reason, on the phone |
| `s2-chase.png` | S2 Chase | **A clear flow that completes the task:** why now, the timeline, and a ready message in the owner's choice of tone, language and channel |
| `s2b-send-sheet.png` | S2 send sheet | **Key product decisions:** the owner taps send, and the sheet is labelled SIMULATED (nothing leaves the phone) |
| `s3-sent.png` | S3 Sent | **A flow within two minutes:** the task ends here; next check, undo, and the timed-run readout |
| `s4-log-reply.png` | S4 Log reply | **How they handle it today, and the friction:** promises are not tracked today; here one tap pauses chasing until the day after |
| `s5-activity.png` | S5 Activity | **How to measure it:** recovered this week, split by link and offline, with a quiet "Paid" line |
| `s6-settings.png` | S6 Settings | **Key product decisions and limits:** quiet hours, the cap of 3, the 48-hour gap, and SIMULATED demo controls |

The screen at 22:30 (quiet hours) is described in the storyboard and plan Appendix E, but not drawn: Home looks like S1 with a banner, and the primary button on S2 reads "Remind me at 9:00 AM".
